DECISION RULES (SPEC §9a, corrected)
  [PASS]  unexplained 0% vs <= 20%
  [PASS]  every requirement-linked element has a real quote (5/5)
  [PASS]  coverage 100%

  Raw traceability is 50%, and that is INFORMATIONAL, not a bar.
  v1 of this spec made it a >= 60% stop condition. Running the instrument showed
  that is wrong: it fails a healthy infrastructure-heavy architecture, where half
  the elements are load balancers and incumbent systems that legitimately have
  no stakeholder requirement. The number that decides is UNEXPLAINED.

  A satisfies-only rule would have rejected 5 of 10 elements.
  Rejecting them is what pressures an architect into inventing requirements.

CAVEAT
  Two, and they matter more than the numbers.
  1. This only means something if the model PREDATES the measurement. Scoring a
     model and evidence authored together measures your own consistency.
  2. 'Legitimately grounded otherwise' is SELF-CLASSIFIED. If you can rationalise
     every box as a standard, unexplained goes to zero and this measures nothing.

  verdict: proceed to §9b
